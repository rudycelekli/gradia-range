# ADR-014: Unsolved campaigns are offered to collaborators

- **Status**: proposed
- **Date**: 2026-10-24
- **Deciders**: Rudy Celekli
- **Tags**: solvers, collaborators, board
- **Recall**: 2026-10-24 decision consult returned unavailable. History is UNKNOWN. Global Ruflo was unavailable.

## Context

The house will not reach every whole-twin objective. A benchmark that only contains objectives the house can already reach is a demonstration of the house. A security report that treats an unreached objective as a pass misstates the attempt. People who can reach those objectives exist, and the Paid room is how they are contracted without inventing a second brand.

## Decision

The house attempts each security campaign first. The attempt is labeled house.

When that attempt's `objective` is 0, the campaign is offered to collaborators: named human solvers and agent authors, on a Paid task, in the same sandbox, under a written scope. The amount is printed on the task. The Open Set remains free and is not cleared to make room for the offer.

A collaborator's standing is theirs. The report to the maintainer still goes out under [ADR-007](ADR-007-report-does-not-wait-on-payment.md), including when the collaborator is the one who reached the objective. Public credit uses the witness. The pitch to a buyer continues to lead with the Rudy Celekli ledger, the Gradia lab, and the report, not with a claim that the house reached the objective.

Collaborators work on twins we operate. An offer is not a request to touch production.

## Consequences

### Positive

- Hard campaigns can still be reached, witnessed, and turned into a report and a fix.
- Solvers are paid for the campaign objective, which is the scarce result.
- The personal brand and the lab stay intact when someone else reaches the objective.

### Negative

- A Paid offer costs money before an enterprise invoice exists. Phase 4 is where that spend is allowed. Phase 1 stays house-only.
- Collaborator scope has to be written per twin. A standing invitation without a named twin and a named task is not a scope.

### Neutral

- Agent authors and humans use the separate boards from [ADR-006](ADR-006-separate-human-and-agent-boards.md). A collaborator can be either kind.
