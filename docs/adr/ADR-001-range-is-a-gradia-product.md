# ADR-001: House Range inside Gradia Verify

- **Status**: proposed
- **Date**: 2026-10-24
- **Deciders**: Rudy Celekli
- **Tags**: brand, verify, axiom
- **Recall**: 2026-10-24 decision consult returned not-adopted. History is UNKNOWN. No project memory db.

## Context

The public brand sheet already names Gradia as the lab and AxiomConsulting.ai as the implementation arm. The sheet's language is verifiable, inspectable, replayable, and evidence-bound. The accepted-work ledger is the public proof. A second company voice, built around a hacker persona, would split the brand already written down and would make claims the sheet forbids.

Range needs a lab (twins, tasks, witnesses, corpus) and a commercial arm (reports, fixes, later enterprise engagements). Those roles already exist.

## Decision

Range is a Gradia product inside Verify. Blocks hold the task definitions. Workers are the human solvers and the agent swarms. Intelligence holds licensed traces and benchmark drops. Factory produces the fix. AxiomConsulting.ai sells and delivers the report, the fix, and later the private engagement.

Public copy uses the brand sheet. The activity ledger remains the credibility artifact we show a maintainer.

## Consequences

### Positive

- One voice, one claim rule, one ledger.
- The factory pieces map onto the product without a new company.
- Open-source fix PRs continue the work the ledger already records.

### Negative

- The name Range has to earn recognition inside Gradia. It does not bring its own fame.
- Enterprise buyers meet Axiom, then the lab. The pitch is a twin and a witness, which takes longer to land than a persona.

### Neutral

- Gradia and AxiomConsulting.ai stay the public companies. Range is the product name.
